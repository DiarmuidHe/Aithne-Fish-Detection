ONC has placed underwater cameras on the seafloor across much of Canada’s coastline on the the Pacific, Atlantic, and Arctic coasts. These cameras operate on timed schedules, offering several minutes of live footage each hour.

Barkley Canyon Axis

Camera is on for five minutes every hour on the hour.

Barkley Canyon Upper Slope

Camera is on for five minutes at the start of every UTC hour.

Campbell River

Camera is on for 5 minutes every hour on the hour.

Cascadia Basin

Camera is on for five minutes every hour on the hour.

China Creek

Camera is on for five minutes every hour on the hour.

Clayoquot Bullseye

Camera is on two schedules:
1) Daily every 4-hours, recordings starting at midnight all the way to 10PM UTC.
2) Camera is on once at 1AM UTC every second day.

Digby Island

Camera is on for five minutes every hour on the hour.

Endeavour Mothra

Camera is on for four minutes every four hours.

Folger Pinnacle

Camera is on for 5 minutes every hour on the hour.

Hartley Bay

Camera is on for five minutes every hour on the hour.

Holyrood Bay

Camera is on for five minutes every hour on the hour.

Kitamaat Village

Camera is on for five minutes every hour on the hour.

Tempo Mini - Main Endeavour Field

Camera records for four minutes every four hours.

The following cameras are offline until further notice:

  • Barkley Canyon Mid-East
  • Barkley Canyon Node Fish Acoustics
