# Gamma addendum — pricing on Card 6, sources appendix

An addition to the deck you already have. Does not regenerate anything. Two paste
blocks: one edits Card 6 in place, one adds a new appendix card after Card 7.

Use it like this:
- **Block A** — open Card 6, choose *Edit with AI* on that card, paste Block A.
- **Block B** — at the end of the deck choose *Add card* > *Paste in text*, paste
  Block B, and leave it as the last card.

Each block restates the theme rules deliberately. Card-level edits in Gamma do not
reliably inherit the deck-level instructions from the original prompt, so the rules
are repeated in full inside each block. Do not trim them.

## Notes before you paste

- **Block A is the one that can go wrong.** Gamma likes to "help" by rounding
  prices, adding a currency symbol of its own, or promoting one of the three
  numbers into a pull-quote. If it does, undo and re-paste with the line "Do not
  create a pull-quote on this card" added to the text rules.
- **Check the prices survived as typed** — €12,000 / €6,000 / €25,000 — and that
  the threshold line still says €50,000. A mangled price is worse than no price.
- **Do not add years or authors to Block B's source lines.** The list is
  deliberately short-form and every entry in it has been verified. Marine Scotland
  is 2016, not 2018; some search results get this wrong.
- **Timing.** Card 6 was budgeted at 45 seconds and now carries three prices; plan
  55 and take the 10 seconds from card 5. The appendix card is not in the run
  order and costs nothing unless you jump to it.
- **The prices are decisions, not findings.** If you are not prepared to sign a
  €12,000 pilot on the day, change the number before you paste — not on stage.
